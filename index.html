About

I am a Deputy Director at the Korea Meteorological Administration. My current work focuses on ocean-climate information, seasonal prediction, verification, and the development of public-facing climate services.

Before joining KMA, I worked as an atmospheric scientist in Korea and the United States. That research background still informs how I approach operational climate information, but this site is intended primarily as an archive of my professional work and projects.
